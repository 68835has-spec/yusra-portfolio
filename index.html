01 — About

About Me.

Ph.D. candidate in Engineering with a strong passion for sustainability and interdisciplinary research spanning water distribution networks, computational fluid dynamics, environmental fluid mechanics, renewable energy conversion systems, and biomaterial engineering. Having served as President and CEO of the Graduate Students’ Association Corporation at the University of Guelph, I represented the entire graduate student body and lead initiatives that advanced academic, professional, and community interests. Areas of expertise include environmental life cycle assessment (LCA), computational fluid dynamics (CFD), renewable energy systems, decentralized water treatment technologies, physical characterization of biomaterials, urban water infrastructure, and engineering design and analysis.
